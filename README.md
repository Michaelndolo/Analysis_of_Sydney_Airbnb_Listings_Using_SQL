## Analysis_of_Sydney_Airbnb_Listings_Using_SQL


### Project overview
---

This being part of the SQL for Data Science course at CoRise, the project was done across two weeks; *Week 1* and *Week 2.* 
I played the role of the General Manager of Airbnb Sydney who was responsible for expanding its Vacation homes and condo rental services all across Australia.

### My Learnings
---

I learnt about the foundations of SQL and mastered SQL basics including filtering and sorting data, aggregations, and complex filtering logic including  GROUP BY and HAVING clauses.
